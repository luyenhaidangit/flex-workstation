# Verification Checklist and Red Flags

## Per-offer checklist (primary source)

- Headline price, billing period, and what the price actually covers (CPU, RAM, disk type, bandwidth, IPv4 included?).
- **Renewal price** and whether the discount applies only to the first term.
- Expiry date and time zone; limited stock?
- Eligibility: new customers only, card or phone required, KYC, region/country limits.
- Hard limits: CPU throttling, shared/burstable cores, traffic caps, port 25 blocked, network speed.
- Refund policy and trial length; what happens at trial end (auto-charge?).
- Data location and backup/support included.
- Provider age and reputation: company registration, abuse/outage history, independent reviews.

## Red flags — drop or flag clearly

- Lifetime plans with no company track record or no stated limits.
- Price far below the provider's own cost tier with no explanation (oversold hardware).
- Renewal price jumps several times above the first term and is hidden in fine print.
- Crypto-only or unusual payment methods with no refund path.
- Free tier that silently converts to a paid plan on a stored card with no spending cap.
- Countdown timers that reset, fake stock counters, "only today" claims that recur.
- Source is an affiliate-only page repeating the headline with no link to the provider terms.
- Requests to disable security, share credentials, or install unknown software to claim the deal.

## Safety cap advice (include when relevant)

For free tiers and credits on pay-as-you-go clouds, tell the user to set a budget alert or spending cap on day one, and note the date credits expire.
